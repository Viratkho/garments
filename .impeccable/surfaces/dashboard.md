# Surface Brief: GarmentFlow Operations Workspace

- **Scope:** Full 12-module internal operations application (`/`, module views)
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate
- **Audience & Job:** DP ENTERPRISES supervisors and owners managing daily garment orders, inventory, stock movements, workers, attendance, salaries, expenses, and P&L.
- **Constraints:** Code-first build (`buildPath: "code"`). Pinned 5-color palette (`#0B0B0F`, `#17171D`, `#8B5CF6`, `#F5F5F0`, `#D50000`), glassmorphism panels, dark/light ink-spread transition, 7 background presets, INR `₹` default, zero paid dependencies.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** GarmentFlow turns DP ENTERPRISES's daily floor operations—bulk stitching orders, fabric stock, worker attendance, and profit/loss—into one glassmorphic precision ledger, refusing generic colorful SaaS template dashboards.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Built strictly from Midnight Black (`#0B0B0F`), Carbon Black (`#17171D`), Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`), Pearl White (`#F5F5F0`), and Rosso Corsa (`#D50000`), using `Manrope` with `tabular-nums`, frosted translucent panels with hairline borders, custom **DP** stitched-thread monogram, and 7 selectable background canvases.
- **STORY:** The operator opens directly into live operational telemetry calculated from real records, switches seamlessly between all 12 modules via a collapsible violet-accented sidebar, records orders/stock/attendance/expenses with instant validation and feedback, and toggles Dark/Light mode via an ink-spread transition originating from the top-bar switch.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Fixed Carbon Black / Pearl White glass sidebar on the left (`260px` desktop, collapsible drawer on mobile) paired with a slim top bar (`DP` logo, current module title, quick search, alert bell popover, glossy ink theme toggle, user badge) and the Dashboard workspace showing key KPI cards, interactive Revenue vs Expense and Order Status charts, Recent Orders, Low Stock Alerts, and Today's Attendance.
- **FORM:** Pinned industrial glassmorphic operations cockpit with organic WebGL2/View-Transition ink-spread theme switching. `seed: none (pinned)` — "Midnight Black: #0B0B0F, Carbon Black: #17171D, Electric Violet: #8B5CF6, Pearl White: #F5F5F0, Rosso Corsa: #D50000 ... premium glassmorphism design language ... ink-spread animation when switching between dark mode and light mode ... 12 main modules".
